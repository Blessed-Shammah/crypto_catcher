# 🤖 CryptoBuddy – Your AI Crypto Advisor

CryptoBuddy is a simple, rule-based Python chatbot designed to give basic cryptocurrency investment advice. Built with beginner-friendly logic and personality, CryptoBuddy offers fun, educational interactions about trending, sustainable, and profitable cryptocurrencies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

CryptoBuddy helps users:

* Understand popular and sustainable cryptocurrencies
* Get investment recommendations based on static data
* Learn about crypto in a casual and friendly way

**⚠️ Disclaimer:** *This chatbot is for educational purposes only. Cryptocurrency investments carry significant risks. Always do your own research before investing!*

---

## 🛠️ Features

### 👤 Personality Design

* Friendly chatbot named **CryptoBuddy**
* Casual tone with emojis 😄
* Fun ASCII art logo
* Randomized greetings and farewells

### 📊 Crypto Knowledge Base

* Predefined database of cryptocurrencies
* Attributes include name, symbol, trend, sustainability, energy usage, and more

### 🧠 Smart Query Handling

* Responds to questions like:

  * "Which crypto is trending?"
  * "Tell me about Ethereum"
  * "What's your best investment pick?"
  * "What are the eco-friendly coins?"

### 💬 User Experience

* Typing animation for realism
* Clear help menu
* Friendly fallback messages when queries aren’t understood

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Save the chatbot as `crypto_buddy.py`
2. Open your terminal or IDE
3. Run the chatbot with:

```bash
python crypto_buddy.py
```

4. Start chatting! Try asking:

* `Which cryptocurrencies are trending up?`
* `What are the most sustainable options?`
* `Tell me about Cardano`
* `What's your investment recommendation?`

---

## 💡 Sample Interactions

```
You: Which crypto is trending up?
CryptoBuddy: Based on current trends, Bitcoin, Cardano and Solana are showing upward price movement! 📈

You: What's the most sustainable coin?
CryptoBuddy: For eco-conscious investing, check out Cardano, Solana, Ripple and Polkadot! 🌱

You: Tell me about Cardano
CryptoBuddy: Here's what I know about Cardano:
Cardano (ADA): A proof-of-stake blockchain platform focused on sustainability and scalability.
- Price Trend: Rising
- Market Cap: Medium
- Energy Use: Low
- Sustainability Score: 8/10

You: What's your investment recommendation?
CryptoBuddy: I'd look at Cardano, Solana and Polkadot. Good potential and energy efficient! 💰🌿
```
